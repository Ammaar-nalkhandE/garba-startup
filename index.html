05 / FAQ

Good questions before a good night.

What is a Garba partner?

A Garba partner is a person you can hire for a defined event or time slot to dance, participate in Garba/Dandiya activities, or support a planned performance according to the agreed booking scope.

Can I book a partner outside my city?

Yes. Use International or a destination-based request for out-of-city and international requirements. Travel, accommodation and related expenses should be agreed separately before confirmation.

What is included in the package price?

The displayed prices are prototype service packages. Your production platform should define exactly what is included, the duration, cancellation policy, taxes, platform fees and any travel charges.

Can I become a Garba partner?

Yes. Partners can create a profile, choose their dance styles, list cities they serve, set availability and submit verification details for review.

How should safety work on the platform?

Keep communication and booking details traceable, verify partners, publish community rules, offer reporting/blocking tools and provide a clear escalation channel for inappropriate behaviour.
